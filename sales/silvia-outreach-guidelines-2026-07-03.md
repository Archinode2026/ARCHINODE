## 2. Rules (non-negotiable, everything else your call)

**Volume**
- **Max 5 emails per day.** Higher rate risks Hotmail spam flag.
- Spread across morning + late afternoon, not one burst.

**Personalization**
- Every email must fill in `[First Name]`, `[Brand Name]`, and one
  concrete detail from the brand's site or LinkedIn (collection name,
  recent award, export markets, etc.). No exceptions.
- 30 seconds of research per brand — skip this and reply rate drops
  70%.

**Tracking**
- After each send, update the xlsx you're working in:
  - Column J `First Contact Date` — YYYY-MM-DD
  - Column K `Response` — leave blank until they reply
- When a reply arrives, keep Column K short — "Interested, call
  scheduled" or "Not exporting Asia now". Long notes waste your time.

**Sending discipline**
- Never re-send the same template to the same brand.
- Template A → 7~10 days no reply → Template B (same email thread,
  "Re: [original subject]"). After that, stop.
- Template C is for brands you know are export-active. Not required.
