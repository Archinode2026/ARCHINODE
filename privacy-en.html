3. Types of Personal Information Collected

We collect personal information from you in the following contexts:

  • Brand Registration: Name, email address, phone number, company name, position, website URL, and referral source
  • Professional Sign-up: Name, email address, company name, job title, industry, and phone number
  • Consultation Request: Contact name, email address, phone number, company name, website, country, area of interest, request details, preferred timing, and budget range
  • Technical File Request (BIM / CAD / catalogue): Name, email address, company name, job title, phone number, and project name, type, scale and timeline
  • Material Sourcing Enquiry: Name, email address, company name, your field of work (optional), category of interest, and enquiry details
  • Brand Enquiry: Name, email address, company name, your field of work (optional), enquiry details, and the brand you contacted
  • Product Quote or Sample Request: Name, email address, company name, your field of work (optional), enquiry details, the product requested, and a shipping address (sample requests only)
  • Newsletter and Launch Alerts: Email address
  • General Communication: Name, email address, subject, and message content when you contact us
  • Automatically Collected: IP address, cookies, access timestamps, and browser or device information
  • Recorded with your enquiry: the fact and time of your consent to the collection and use of your personal data, and your account identifier if you were signed in

4. Purpose of Collection and Use

We collect and process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To manage your brand listing and registration on ARCHINODE
  • To create and maintain your brand portal account
  • To communicate with you about your account, listings, and services
  • To send newsletters and updates you have subscribed to
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service
  • To analyze usage patterns and optimize our website performance
  • To receive and answer general enquiries, material sourcing enquiries, brand enquiries, and product quote or sample requests
  • To receive consultation requests and carry out the consultation
  • To review and fulfil technical file requests (BIM / CAD / catalogue)
  • To record and keep proof of your consent to the collection and use of your personal data (Personal Information Protection Act, Art. 22)

5. Retention Period

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Specifically:

  • Information related to your brand account will be retained until you request deletion of your account
  • Information from inactive accounts will be retained for up to 3 years after your last activity
  • Newsletter subscription data will be retained until you unsubscribe
  • Enquiries, sourcing enquiries and consultation requests will be retained for 3 years after they are closed (E-Commerce Act Enforcement Decree, Article 6 — records of consumer complaints and dispute resolution)
  • Information we must keep by law is retained as follows: records of advertising and labelling — 6 months; records of contracts and withdrawal of subscription — 5 years; records of payment and supply of goods — 5 years; records of consumer complaints and dispute resolution — 3 years (all under the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce); website access logs — 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act)

5A. How We Destroy Personal Information

When the retention period ends or the purpose of processing is achieved and the personal information is no longer needed, we destroy it without delay.

  • Procedure: We identify the information to be destroyed and destroy it with the approval of our Privacy Officer. Where another law requires retention, we keep it separately for that period and destroy it when the period ends.
  • Method: Electronic files are permanently erased by technical means that make recovery impossible. Printed documents are shredded or incinerated.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. However, we may share your information with service providers in the following circumstances:

  • Firebase (Google): We use Firebase for user authentication and hosting services. Your email and basic profile information may be processed by Firebase according to Google's privacy policy
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Brands and manufacturers listed on ARCHINODE (located in Europe and elsewhere): When you send a quote, sample, technical-data (BIM/CAD) or brand enquiry about a specific brand or product, we pass your enquiry to that brand so it can respond. Recipient: the brand and its manufacturer (mostly in Europe). Purpose: replying to the enquiry, quoting, shipping samples, approving technical data. Items: name, company, email, phone, industry, enquiry text, project details and — for sample requests — your shipping address. Retained until the enquiry or transaction is closed (or longer where the law requires). This happens only when you send such an enquiry yourself; if you prefer not to share, do not send the enquiry — the brand will then not reply or ship.
  • Service Providers: The specific processors we engage and the work entrusted to them are listed in Section 6A below. Transfers outside Korea are described in Section 6B.

7. Rights of Data Subjects

Under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Correction: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Right to Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent to the collection and use of your personal information at any time
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request restrictions on how we process your information
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your information in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at office@archinode.org. We will respond to your request within 10 days in accordance with applicable law.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

ARCHINODE uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Specifically:

  • Analytical Cookies: We use Google Analytics 4 to measure how the website is used. It collects your IP address (with the last octet masked), browser and device information, the pages you visit, time on page, and how you arrived. No directly identifying information such as your name or e-mail is sent. These cookies run only if you consent to "Analytics" in the cookie banner; if you decline, none are set. The statistics are kept under Google Analytics' data-retention settings — event data for 2 months and user data such as cookies and user identifiers for 14 months, with the 14 months restarting whenever the same visitor is active again. You can change your choice in the banner at any time.
  • Functional Cookies: We use functional cookies to remember your preferences and improve your experience
  • Disabling Cookies: You can disable or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers include instructions for managing cookies in their help menus

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features on our website.

9. Data Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, and destruction:

  • Encrypted Transmission: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) technology
  • Access Controls: Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Secure Storage: We use secure servers and databases to store your information
  • Regular Updates: We regularly update and maintain our security systems to address new threats

We faithfully carry out the technical, administrative and physical safeguards required by Article 29 of the Personal Information Protection Act and Article 30 of its Enforcement Decree.
